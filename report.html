Report or Remove Information

If a search result is incorrect, outdated, harmful, or contains information that should not be displayed, please contact the site administrator for review.

What to include

  • The page or result you are reporting
  • A short explanation of the issue
  • What correction or removal you are requesting
  • Proof of authorization where reasonably necessary

Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, or unnecessary identity documents.

Contact Support on WhatsApp

Requests are reviewed in good faith. We may ask for additional information before making a change.
