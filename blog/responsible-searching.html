Responsible Use of Search Tools

By Bunty Search Hub · September 11, 2026

Search tools should help people find information they are allowed to access, not expose private data or enable harassment. Use a tool only for a legitimate purpose and follow applicable laws and service rules.

Respect other people

Do not publish another person's phone number, identity information, address, or search result without permission. If a result is incorrect or harmful, contact the administrator and request a correction or removal.
