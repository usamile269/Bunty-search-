How to Protect Your Personal Information Online

By Bunty Search Hub · September 11, 2026

Personal information can travel quickly once posted online. Before sharing a phone number, identity document, address, or screenshot, ask whether the recipient needs it and whether the channel is trustworthy.

Simple protections

  • Use a unique password and two-factor authentication for important accounts.
  • Review app permissions and remove access that is no longer needed.
  • Avoid sending OTPs, recovery codes, or identity documents through casual messages.
  • Delete old public posts that reveal your address, routine, or private contacts.

Small privacy habits reduce risk. If your information is exposed, change affected passwords, contact the relevant service, and keep evidence of suspicious activity.
