How to Handle Unknown Calls Safely

By Bunty Search Hub · September 11, 2026

An unknown caller may be harmless, but urgent requests for money, verification codes, or account access are warning signs. Do not share an OTP or click a link during an unexpected call.

What to do

End the call and verify the organisation through its official website or a known number. Block repeated spam, report fraud to the relevant provider, and save evidence without forwarding it to others.

Never let pressure or a caller's claimed authority replace independent verification.
