Strong Passwords and Safer Accounts

By Bunty Search Hub · September 11, 2026

A strong password is long, unique, and not reused across important accounts. A password manager can create and store different passwords so one breach does not expose every account.

Protect account recovery

Turn on two-factor authentication, keep recovery details current, review active sessions, and never share a password, recovery code, or OTP—even if a message appears to come from support.
