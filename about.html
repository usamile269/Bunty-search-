About Bunty Search Hub

Bunty Search Hub is a simple, fast search interface designed to help authorized users find information through one convenient dashboard.

Our goal

We aim to provide a clear and accessible search experience with a responsive design, useful result actions, and straightforward account access.

Responsible use

Use this service only for lawful purposes and only with information you are authorized to access. Do not use it to harass, impersonate, discriminate against, or harm another person. We may restrict access where abuse or unauthorized use is suspected.

Features

  • Simple search dashboard
  • Mobile-friendly interface
  • Result sharing and utility actions
  • Premium tools where enabled
